Data Governance & User Control

Account & Data Deletion

Effective: October 2026 • Compliant with Google Play & Apple Store Policies
Your Data, Your Choice: SXG Companion empowers you with complete control over your information. You can delete your entire account or selectively request deletion of specific parts of your cloud data (such as cloud presets, setlists, or authentication profiles) at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Fastest)

If you have the SXG Companion app installed on your Android or iOS device, you can permanently wipe your account or cloud-synced items immediately without waiting for manual processing:

  • 1

    Open App Settings

    Launch SXG Companion and tap the gear icon in the top header to enter Settings.

  • 2

    Select Account & Cloud Sync

    Tap your profile or navigate to the Account & Cloud Sync section.

  • 3

    Choose What to Delete

    Select either Clear Synced Presets / Setlists (partial wipe) or Delete Account Permanently (complete wipe).

  • 4

    Confirm

    Confirm the deletion prompt. Your data is removed from the cloud database instantly.

Data Retention & Policy Details

What is Permanently Deleted?

  • Authentication Information: Your unique User ID, email address, and linked OAuth tokens are permanently scrubbed from the identity database.
  • Cloud Musical Presets: All uploaded registration parameters, style assignments, voice split maps, and tempo overrides.
  • Setlists & Charts: All cloud-synced song orders, key notations, and chord charts.

What is NOT Retained?

SXG Companion does not collect or retain advertising identifiers, tracking telemetry, financial data, or musical audio recordings. We do not sell or share any user data with third-party data brokers.

Local Data on Your Device

Submitting a cloud deletion request purges your information from the remote servers. If the SXG Companion app is still installed on your phone or tablet, local cached files remain inside your device's isolated application sandbox until you clear the app data or uninstall the app.
