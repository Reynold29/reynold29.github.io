Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026 • Effective Immediately
Summary: SXG Companion is built with a privacy-first ethos. We do not sell personal data, track behavioral telemetry, or include advertising SDKs. When you choose to use our cloud accounts, your information is used solely to authenticate you and securely synchronize your custom song presets and setlists across your devices. You retain full rights to delete your entire account or specific parts of your data at any time.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how SXG Companion ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Reynold (Reyzie), treats information when you use our mobile application on Android and iOS devices.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

SXG Companion is designed with an offline-first architecture. We collect only what is strictly necessary to deliver user-requested features:

A. Offline Stage Operation (No Account Required)

You can use SXG Companion completely offline without creating an account. In offline mode, all style configurations, chord charts, and presets remain strictly on your physical device. We collect zero personal data in this mode.

B. Optional User Accounts & Cloud Sync

If you choose to create an account (via Google Sign-In or email) to back up and sync your library across devices:

  • Account Identifiers: We store your email address, display name, and unique user ID provided by authentication services solely to authenticate your session and protect your library.
  • User Musical Content: We store your custom song presets (tempo, style assignments, voice split maps) and gig setlists (song order, chord charts) in secure cloud databases so they stay synchronized across your phones and tablets.
  • Security & Encryption: All cloud communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS protocols.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect location data or GPS coordinates.
  • We do not collect device identifiers for advertising (IDFA, GAID).
  • We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or telemetry tracking libraries.
  • We do not record or transmit audio, microphone inputs, or raw MIDI SysEx dumps played on your keyboard.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or musical data.

4. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

SXG Companion requests only the minimal permissions necessary to communicate with your musical keyboard and establish local Air Sync connections:

USB & MIDI Host Access (android.hardware.usb.host)

Used exclusively to communicate with compatible Yamaha arranger keyboards (PSR-SX and Genos series) over a physical USB cable attached to the [USB TO HOST] port. MIDI messages (such as style selection, chord triggers, tempo, and voice switches) are transmitted directly between your mobile device and the instrument.

Local Network & Wi-Fi Multicast (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE)

Used exclusively by the Air Sync feature to discover other peer devices on your phone's personal hotspot or local stage network via UDP broadcast and establish a direct peer-to-peer TCP connection. No live performance data is sent over the wider internet.

Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE)

On Android, when you host an Air Sync session, the app runs a lightweight foreground service. This keeps the Wi-Fi radio and CPU awake so the musical connection does not disconnect when your phone screen turns off or locks during a live rehearsal or performance.

Local Storage & Documents

Used to save, load, and export your personal song presets, setlist configurations, and custom style mappings locally on your device.

5. Account & Data Deletion (User Control)

We firmly believe in your right to data sovereignty and transparency. You have the right to request deletion of your entire account or specific portions of your data at any time:

  • In-App Immediate Deletion: Open SXG Companion, navigate to Settings > Account & Cloud Sync, and tap Delete Account & Cloud Data. This permanently purges your account and cloud library immediately.
  • Selective Data Deletion: You can choose to delete only your cloud presets, only your setlists, or your entire account without affecting other files.
  • Web Deletion Portal: If you uninstalled the application or prefer to request deletion via the web, visit our dedicated portal:
    SXG Companion Account & Data Deletion Page →

Web requests are processed and confirmed within 7 business days. Upon deletion, all associated authentication records, cloud presets, and setlists are permanently removed from our active databases and cannot be restored.

6. Children's Privacy

SXG Companion is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Third-Party Links & Stores

The app is distributed through official application marketplaces (Google Play Store, Apple App Store / TestFlight). Those platforms operate under their own independent privacy policies for app installation and crash diagnostics.
