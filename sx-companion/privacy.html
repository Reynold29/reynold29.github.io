Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026 • Effective Immediately
Summary: SX Companion is built with a privacy-first ethos. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal information, telemetry, or analytics. Everything happens locally on your device and between your connected hardware.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how SX Companion ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Reynold (Reyzie), treats information when you use our mobile application on Android and iOS devices.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization. When you use SX Companion:

  • We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide an email address.
  • We do not collect location data or GPS coordinates.
  • We do not collect device identifiers for advertising (IDFA, GAID).
  • We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or analytical telemetry libraries.
  • We do not record or transmit the musical content, notes, SysEx messages, or audio produced by your keyboard.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

SX Companion requests only the minimal permissions necessary to communicate with your musical keyboard and establish local Air Sync connections:

USB & MIDI Host Access (android.hardware.usb.host)

Used exclusively to communicate with the Yamaha PSR-SX keyboard over a physical USB cable attached to the [USB TO HOST] port. MIDI messages (such as style selection, chord triggers, tempo, and voice switches) are transmitted directly between your mobile device and the instrument.

Local Network & Wi-Fi Multicast (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE)

Used exclusively by the Air Sync feature to discover other peer devices on your phone's personal hotspot or local stage network via UDP broadcast and establish a direct peer-to-peer TCP connection. No data is sent over the wider internet.

Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE)

On Android, when you host an Air Sync session, the app runs a lightweight foreground service. This keeps the Wi-Fi radio and CPU awake so the musical connection does not disconnect when your phone screen turns off or locks during a live rehearsal or performance.

Local Storage & Documents

Used to save, load, and export your personal song presets, setlist configurations, and custom style mappings locally on your device.

4. Data Storage & Security

All app preferences, presets, and setlists are saved exclusively in your device's local application storage sandbox. We do not operate external cloud database servers. If you delete the app, your locally stored data is removed unless you have exported a JSON backup to your own files or cloud drive.

5. Children's Privacy

SX Companion does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Third-Party Links & Stores

The app is distributed through official application marketplaces (Google Play Store, Apple App Store / TestFlight). Those platforms operate under their own independent privacy policies for app installation and crash diagnostics.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically if new features require changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
