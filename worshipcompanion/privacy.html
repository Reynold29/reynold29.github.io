Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026 • Effective Immediately
Official Google Sites Mirror: This policy is synchronized with our hosted Google Sites privacy policy.
Open Google Sites Mirror →
Summary: Worship Companion is built for worship leaders, choir members, and church musicians with an offline-first, privacy-respecting foundation. We do not sell your personal data, track your location, or request broad access to your device's photo gallery.

1. Overview

Worship Companion ("we," "our," or "us"), created by Reynold (Reyzie), is committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information and device permissions are managed when you use the Worship Companion mobile applications (Android and iOS), desktop editions, and web lyrics reader.

2. System Photo Picker & Media Access

In strict compliance with Google Play Store policies and Apple App Store guidelines, Worship Companion utilizes the Native System Photo Picker:

  • Zero Broad Media Permissions: The app does not request or require persistent, broad storage permissions such as READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.
  • User-Directed Selection: When you choose to customize your profile picture or import a picture of a hymn sheet for OCR extraction, the secure operating system dialog is invoked. The app only receives temporary access to the specific single file you choose.
  • No Background Gallery Scanning: Worship Companion never scans, indexes, or browses your personal photo albums or private files.

3. Camera Permission & Usage

The application requests access to your device's camera exclusively when you explicitly tap the AI Hymnal Scanner feature.

  • Purpose: To capture a real-time photo of printed song sheets, hymnal pages, or church service bulletins for instant lyric extraction.
  • Live User Control: The camera is only active while the scanner viewfinder is open on your screen. No background photo capture, audio recording, or video tracking ever occurs.

4. Information You Provide to Us

A. Account Information (Optional)

Worship Companion can be used completely anonymously as an offline songbook without an account. If you choose to enable cloud backup and multi-device synchronization via Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture avatar provided by the authentication provider.

B. Custom User Content & Setlists

When you create setlists (playlists), mark songs as favorites, set custom key transpose offsets, or submit new hymn lyrics to our community review queue, this information is tied to your account to keep your devices in sync.

5. How We Use and Process Information

We process data solely for direct functional capabilities:

  • To display and search through the 2,000+ hymn and song library across Kannada, English, and other languages.
  • To synchronize your personal worship setlists across your phone, tablet, and web reader.
  • To extract readable text from scanned song sheet images using optical character recognition (OCR).
  • To review and verify user-submitted songs prior to adding them to the global public database.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure & AI Processing

We partner only with industry-leading, secure infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: Serves as our encrypted backend database and authentication layer (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security). Your synced setlists and profile information are stored safely here.
  • Google Gemini / Cloud Vision API: When you use the AI Image Scanner, captured song sheet images are transmitted over TLS-encrypted connections solely for in-flight text recognition. Images are not retained or utilized to train general AI models.
  • Google & Apple Authentication: Verifies your login without storing plaintext passwords on our servers.

7. Local Offline Storage & Encryption

Worship Companion is engineered with an offline-first philosophy. The entire core hymn catalog, chord charts, and metadata are bundled and cached locally using industry-standard SQLCipher database encryption. You can lead worship in remote churches, retreats, and venues with zero internet connectivity.

8. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have absolute ownership and control over your personal data:

  • One-Tap In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated cloud records at any time directly within the app by navigating to Profile > Settings > Delete Account.
  • Instant Permanent Purge: When requested, your authentication credentials, cloud-synced playlists, and profile records are immediately purged from our Supabase backend.
  • Local Data Wipe: Uninstalling the application or clearing app data immediately wipes all local caches, keys, and saved offline records from your device.

9. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to our services, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove the data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new regulatory requirements, or operating system policy changes. We will notify users by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and within the app's settings section.
