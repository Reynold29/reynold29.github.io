Data & Account Deletion Portal

Compliant with Google Play Store Data Safety & Apple App Store Policy · Response within 24–48 hours

Delete Data Without Deleting Your Account: You are not required to delete your entire parish member account to remove specific data. You may request the deletion of individual data components (such as your residential GPS location pin, carol navigation directions, or uploaded family photos) while keeping your church directory account and digital receipt access fully intact.

1 Method A: Instant In-App Deletion (Fastest)

If you have the YKC One app installed on your smartphone:

  1. Open YKC One and navigate to More or Family Settings.
  2. Select Account & Privacy.
  3. Choose either "Clear Home Geolocation Pin" (partial wipe) or "Delete Account & Access" (full wipe).
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your credentials and stored device session are immediately cleared.
