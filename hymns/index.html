i.In the app

Built for the pew, the choir loft and the organ bench.

Everything you reach for during a service, in one app that stays out of the way. Try the language switch on this hymn.

  • Kannada and English

    Switch any hymn between Kannada and English with one tap. Set the text size you can read, and pick up where you left off.

  • Sing along with audio

    MIDI playback with live transpose, tempo control and a separate instrument for soprano, alto, tenor and bass. Play it through a speaker with AirPlay or Chromecast.

  • Read it like a book

    Scroll through the stanzas, or turn on Page Flip and turn pages with your finger. Hide the controls when you are leading worship.

  • Order of Service

    The Regular Sunday and Festival liturgies in Kannada and English, with a full-screen reader and jump-to-page.

  • Mangalore Tunes

    The full M.T. hymn book, with its own search, categories and favourites, one tap away from the CSI books.

  • Find any hymn fast

    Search by number, title or meter, then jump straight to a meter in the list. No more flipping pages during the announcement.

  • Favourites and collections

    Star hymns and build collections for each occasion. Sign in and they follow you to every device.

  • Works offline

    Lyrics ship inside the app and update quietly in the background, so it still works in a church hall with no signal.

  • Christmas mode

    A festive theme every December, with community carols and carol-book PDFs shared by churches.

iv.Why this app

Why this app exists

The old hymn apps stopped getting updates and started vanishing from the Play Store. So I built one that would stay.

The apps our congregations relied on were Android-only, looked their age and were slowly being pulled from the store. This one puts the hymns, keerthanes, Mangalore Tunes and Order of Service in one place, in both languages, with audio to sing along to.

Now it is a native app on both iPhone and Android, built to last.

Reynold Preetham Developer. MCA student at Jain University and cyber-security enthusiast, building this in spare time.

I am not an app developer by trade. I learned from YouTube and the community, first in Flutter. Both apps are now rebuilt natively: SwiftUI on iPhone and iPad, Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on Android. Suggestions and contributions are always welcome.
