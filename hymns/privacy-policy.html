Hymns Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 11/08/2025 (August 11th, 2025)

WE value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we manage information related to your use of the app.

Information We Collect:

  • Internet Connection Status: The app checks for an active internet connection and uses it to stream hymn audio and download app and lyrics updates.
  • Notification Permission: Required to keep you informed about new features, hymn updates, and important announcements.
  • Audio Playback: The app uses your device's speaker to stream hymn audio. No audio is recorded or transmitted.
  • Vibration Access: Used solely for haptic feedback to enhance user interaction.
  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information such as your name, email, phone number, location, or device data.
  • Account Information: If you choose to create an account using email and password or Google Sign-In, we collect:
    • Your email address
    • Your Google ID (if signing in with Google)
    This information is used only to create and manage your account. It is encrypted and securely stored. We do not share this data with any third parties.

How We Use the Information:

  • Update checks: We use your Internet connection status solely to determine if we can download and install the latest app and lyrics updates.
  • Stream Audio: We also use your internet to stream hymn audio, and nothing else. No audio is recorded and microphone isn't accessed.
  • Account Management: Your email or Google ID is used only to authenticate and manage your account.

Information Storage and Security:

  • Local + Secure Cloud Storage: Most data is stored locally on your device. For account-related features, email and credentials are securely stored in encrypted format using industry-standard authentication systems.
  • No Data Sharing: We do not share your personal data with any third-party services.
  • No Ads or Analytics SDKs: We do not use third-party SDKs that collect personal information.

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at reyziecrafts@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within 7 days.

Third-Party Services:

Our app does not use any third-party services that collect or process your information.

Changes to this Policy:

This Privacy Policy may be subject to change in the future. We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on our website or within the app.

Contact Us:

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact me at: reyziecrafts@gmail.com
