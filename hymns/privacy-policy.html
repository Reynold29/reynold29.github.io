Privacy policy

We value your privacy. This policy explains what the CSI Hymns Book app uses, why, and how to have your data deleted.

Effective 11 August 2025

1.Information we collect

  • Internet connection status. The app checks for an active connection and uses it to stream hymn audio and download app and lyric updates.
  • Notification permission. Used to tell you about new features, hymn updates and important announcements.
  • Audio playback. The app uses your device's speaker to play hymn audio. No audio is recorded or transmitted.
  • Vibration. Used only for haptic feedback when you interact with the app.
  • No personal data by default. We do not collect or store personally identifiable information such as your name, email, phone number, location or device data.
  • Account information. If you choose to create an account with email and password or Google Sign-In, we collect:
    • your email address
    • your Google ID, if you sign in with Google
    This is used only to create and manage your account. It is encrypted, stored securely and never shared with third parties.

2.How we use it

  • Update checks. Your connection status is used only to decide whether the app can download the latest app and lyric updates.
  • Audio streaming. Your connection is used to stream hymn audio and nothing else. The microphone is never accessed.
  • Account management. Your email or Google ID is used only to sign you in and manage your account.

3.Storage and security

  • Local and secure cloud storage. Most data stays on your device. For account features, your email and credentials are stored encrypted using industry-standard authentication systems.
  • No data sharing. We do not share your personal data with third-party services.
  • No ads or analytics SDKs. We do not use third-party SDKs that collect personal information.
You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted at any time, using the data deletion form or by emailing reyziecrafts@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 7 days.

4.Third-party services

The app does not use any third-party services that collect or process your information.

5.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be posted on this page or announced in the app.

6.Contact

Questions about this policy? Email reyziecrafts@gmail.com.
